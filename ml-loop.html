The ML loop today

This is a deliberately incomplete loop. The safeguards that are active are visible; the pieces that could change a model remain dark or offline.

VERIFIED

Five stages with different permissions

The model in development looks at a produce photo and can answer several classification questions at once. Nothing related to freshness ships: the mobile build scanner flag is off, and scanner routes need a bound release manifest. The pipeline below shows the evidence boundaries around future improvement work.

LIVE

Consent captured

PhotoTrainingConsentView.swift shows a consent screen before a first photo can leave the device. It starts off, gives accept and decline equal visual weight, works for guests, and saves the local choice immediately.

LIVE

Consent read back

UserSettingsService.swift refuses upload until the server acknowledges the exact local choice. training_consent.py refuses training writes without a live-consent lease and checks again during work.

LIVE

Eligibility

training_eligibility.py accepts only server-adjudicated human review for training rows. A model prediction, including an automatic label, is never its own training label.

DARK

Schedulers

Corrections export, auto-labeling, and retraining are built and tested behind flags that default off. The improvement health route reports each stage instead of letting a dormant path become invisible.

OFFLINE

Human-gated promotion

model_promotion_lock.py allows one human-called promotion path. It rechecks consent revisions, candidate evidence, a gold-set verdict, and a canary report. force=true is rejected.

Notice that consent is verified before data moves, while any step that could change what people receive stays separated from automatic operation.

REPORTED

What is being built next

The self-improving loop is built and tested behind flags that default off. It is not presented here as a live feature.

INFERRED

The ethic in one paragraph

Consent is treated as a living fact, not a checkbox: captured before use, echoed to the server, checked again when needed, honored across devices, and revoked with deletion. The dangerous half of the loop can be built while promotion to people stays human. The moral rule is encoded in leases, tombstones, and filters in training_consent.py, not left only in a policy document.
