AI

The AI names your food. It never guesses freshness.

Today a vision model identifies what's in the photo, and identification is all it does. Freshness comes from data, not from a guess. See the ML loop and its consent boundaries.

🧠

Vision AI

Looks at the photo, returns food names. Identification runs on a third-party model; in-house candidates are being evaluated but none has been promoted, and no fallback between the two is implemented.

in: photoout: "milk, lettuce, eggs"runs: per scan
🔬

The evidence gate

Before any in-house model ships, it must pass hard safety gates: at most 1 in 100 truly-spoiled items may be called fresh on the release evidence set (1 in 50 under each hard lighting or angle condition); a separate promotion check tolerates up to 1 in 10. None has cleared the bar yet; until one does, identification runs on a third-party model and nothing judges freshness.

in: candidate modelout: ship / not yetruns: before any launch
🤝 The honesty rule: if we can't back an answer with data, the app says "we don't know" instead of making something up. That rule costs us features, and it's why you can trust the ones we keep.

THE ROBOTS

The automations in between

Nobody has to remember to check anything. Each robot has one job, a trigger, and a place it reports to. Read the full automation inventory.

See the ledger for what was checked this week.

🚀

Release robot

A human approves and starts every deploy. The robot builds it, rehearses it on the twin, and undoes any failure automatically.

trigger: one approval
build once → rehearse on the twin →
same build to production →
any failure = automatic undo
🛡️

Gatekeeper

Blocks any pull request that fails release contracts, the external-review gate or shopper tests. The data-quality gate is the exception: both of its jobs skip on pull requests entirely.

in: every push/PRout: green or blockedtrigger: each push/PR
🍎

Store watcher

Watches the App Store review status so nobody refreshes a dashboard.

out: chat alerttrigger: every 30 min
🩺

Health watcher

Makes 5 bounded network probes and 3 local freshness checks every 30 minutes, and alerts only on a state change. The probes cover production health, production readiness, the canary twin, the interns proxy and the custom domain; the local checks cover backup verification, App Store watcher freshness and supervisor freshness.

out: page a humantrigger: every 30 min
