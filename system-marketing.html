Job in the loop

Help the right person understand the product.

Marketing turns a founder's problem statement into a truthful invitation. It explains what the product can do today, listens to what people still need, and sends that evidence back to business and product.

Source capability
The private default branch contains static marketing pages and related site assets. Existence is not publication, reach or accuracy.
Runtime evidence
This lecture makes no claim about traffic, conversion, audience size or current campaigns because no current denominator is linked here.
Human gate
A person verifies every outward claim, audience, destination and final publish action.

How it works

Listen, explain, invite.

01 / Listen

Find a repeated problem

Questions, support notes and observed behavior reveal a problem people are already trying to solve.

02 / Frame

Explain only what is supported

Each sentence matches current product capability and clearly states a relevant limit.

03 / Invite

Invite a response

A person chooses the audience and destination, then listens to responses instead of assuming attention proves value.

Automation and tests

Draft quickly. Verify slowly.

  • Agents can research themes, create variants and check links or language.They must not invent audience metrics, outcomes, customer proof or publication receipts.
  • Content gates scan public files and inspect responsive presentation.The hub validator covers prohibited terms and evidence vocabulary; browser review covers readability and links.
  • Publishing is a representational act.The owner approves the exact copy, channel and timing. A queued draft is not a published message.

Critical boundary

A draft is not a market signal.

Generated words are cheap; earned evidence is scarce. Marketing quality comes from matching the message to what the product can actually deliver and what an audience actually does.

Never inferA page in source, a scheduled draft or a platform preview does not prove publication, reach, response or conversion.
RequireClaim citations, explicit owner approval, provider readback and denominated audience evidence before reporting outcomes.

How it improves

Let response sharpen the problem.

Signal

A question, objection or observed task result is captured without inflating it.

Evidence

Audience, delivery and response counts stay together so a result has context.

Proposal

Change one message

An agent suggests a tighter promise or clearer invitation tied to current product truth.

Decision

The owner decides whether and where to speak again.
