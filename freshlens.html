FreshLens

The FreshLens target is simple: point your camera at a fridge, basket, or produce shelf and score every item in one scene. Then turn the advisory result into action: rescue what’s turning, or mark it down before it’s lost. The real YOLO/live-service path is not yet reproducible in a clean checkout.

Current mission: truth, automation, app practice, handoff. — Agents may gather and propose; deterministic gates verify; humans control product decisions, model promotion, merges, deployment, publication, and value movement.

Python FastAPI PyTorch YOLO / Ultralytics Claude API JavaScript

The leap

FreshLens is the 10x target: scan the whole scene at once. The prototype and offline pieces exist; reproducible real-detector, representative model/data, and live-service proof remain open.

That single capability serves both sides of the business. Pointed at a shopper’s fridge, it’s a magic “what do I eat first, and what can I cook tonight?” moment. Pointed at a store’s produce section, it’s an instant freshness audit that tells the manager exactly what to mark down—the heart of the markdown-automation pitch.

This summer’s build: Snap-to-Claim

On top of the FreshLens base, the team builds one focused feature for Weeks 3–8: Snap-to-Claim (Return Trust) — a capped, evidence-backed, human-review claim and recovery loop for perishables that arrive spoiled.

In the local candidate, a shopper submits item and receipt evidence. The freshness model advises on condition; deterministic cap and duplicate checks run before it; and a separately authorized, assigned-store reviewer records any approval or decline. The resulting decision is bound to a signed artifact and tamper-evident audit trail. The wedge is not the freshness score; it is the closed, verifiable-evidence loop. No refund, credit, payout, or other issuance occurs.

1

Advise

The model scores the photo 0–100 on the canonical scale (0 = fresh, 100 = spoiled/urgent). It’s an advisory input, never a guarantee — copy never says “our AI confirms this is spoiled”.

2

Decide (capped)

The current RE-SCOPE gate disables automatic approval and routes every otherwise eligible case to an assigned human reviewer. A rolling $15 / 30-day and $10 / claim ceiling bounds any recorded approval; deterministic cap and duplicate gates still fail closed.

3

Prove

The local candidate now creates one immutable signed decision artifact and verifies its mock-anchor state. Durable storage, external trust, and money issuance remain separate blocked capabilities.

Four guardrails are non-negotiable: advisory language only · human review for every otherwise eligible case under the current gate · a separately approved calibration gate before any auto-threshold · cap + duplicate checks before the model. Full detail in INITIATIVE-freshness-guarantee.md and the executable SPEC-claim-loop.md.

→
5 · ProveThe local prototype binds the decision, verifies its mock anchor, and exports an account-scoped report.

Mind the direction. The canonical FreshLens scale is 0 = perfectly fresh, 100 = spoiled / urgent (low = green/fresh, high = red/use-now). The raw model returns the opposite (high = fresh); the es_api adapter inverts it at the seam, so all claim code works on the canonical scale.

Target architecture

A FastAPI service that puts a capped, audited policy around the freshness model. The current local candidate remains human-review-only and verifies the receipt-binding/mock-anchor boundary; it is not a durable production or issuance architecture.
