Refrigerator inventory · contract first

Multi-capture is a system, not a bigger prompt.

A useful fridge inventory requires coverage guidance, instance evidence across views, identity abstention, a strict condition-eligibility gate, receipt provenance, one correction surface, and candidate-specific evaluation.

Source truth: the intern sandbox predates this pivot and is UNBOUND. Its old scanner, claim, provider, and model paths are not this architecture.

End-to-end data flow

One physical item can appear in several frames. The system keeps raw observations, cross-view tracks, item hypotheses, condition signals, and the user's final inventory distinct.

01 · CAPTURE

Guided sweep

Prompt shelf, door, and drawer coverage. Measure blur, glare, occlusion, and overlap before accepting frames.

02 · OBSERVE

Segment instances

Emit per-frame masks or boxes with quality and detection confidence. Do not convert a class label directly into inventory.

03 · TRACK

Join views

Associate the same physical instance across frames. Expose uncertain merges and splits instead of hiding count errors.

04 · IDENTIFY

Classify or abstain

Return taxonomy version, alternatives, confidence, and an OOD reason. Unknown is a supported result.

05 · QUALIFY

Gate visible condition

Score only supported produce with enough visible surface and acceptable image quality. Packaged or occluded items remain unscored.

06 · RECONCILE

Add receipt evidence

Match synthetic receipt lines to item candidates with confidence and provenance. Conflicts stay visible.

07 · REVIEW

Correct once

Let the shopper edit, merge, split, remove, add, and confirm. Unknown age never creates a fabricated deadline.

08 · PERSIST

Store provenance

Each confirmed field records its source: capture, receipt, user edit, policy default, or unknown.

09 · LEARN SAFELY

Separate eligibility

Immediate processing, retention, analytics, and training eligibility are four different consent and policy states.

Evaluation that matches the product

Split by refrigerator and capture session, not frame. Near-duplicate views in train and test would make a weak model look strong.

Coverage and capture

Usable-frame rate, missing-region rate, reshoots, median sweep time, and frames uploaded.

Instances and tracking

Scene coverage recall, precision at a frozen match rule, count error, duplicate rate, merges/splits, track purity, and identity switches.

Identity and OOD

Supported top-1/top-3 accuracy, taxonomy coverage, unknown false accepts, abstention, and small/occluded slices.

Visible condition

Eligibility precision/recall, calibration error, balanced error, and false-safe rate on eligible visible produce only.

Receipts and provenance

Line parsing, match precision/recall, conflict rate, abstention, and preservation of source per field.

Product and runtime

Time/actions to first confirmed inventory, corrections, abandonment, accessibility, p50/p95/p99 latency, retries, cost, and partial recovery.

No invented threshold: freeze representative, privacy-clean evaluation data and record the baseline before approving release floors. Any prompt, taxonomy, model, threshold, interface, or candidate-SHA change reruns affected gates.

External evidence without surveillance

Receipts

Prefer in-app upload, share sheet, or a user-specific forwarding address. A receipt can support identity, quantity, and purchase time; it cannot prove current presence, opened date, expiry, or condition.

Email

Broad mailbox access is not a shortcut. A future connector needs separate scope, retention, deletion, security, and product review. Marketing permission remains separate.

Training data

Capture does not equal training consent. Eligibility is server-owned, versioned, auditable, revocable per artifact, and distinct from immediate processing, retention, and analytics.

Observability

Events carry opaque IDs, versions, stage outcomes, latency, and reason codes. No raw image, receipt text, email content, or user-entered label appears in analytics.

Source and release handoff

When the owner declares an exact candidate, approved paths are mapped by candidate and sandbox SHA, per-file SHA-256, contract version, and a content-attested manifest. A second owner verifies every digest and signature reference.

Intern authority

Build and review in the sandbox using synthetic fixtures. Report missing canonical seams as BLOCKED. No production credentials or release actions.

Owner authority

Bind safe source, transfer reviewed changes, create source-linked builds, deploy, upload TestFlight, submit, and verify production through separate gates.

One remaining binding action: after the pivot candidate exists, the owner must generate the content-addressed source map for that exact SHA and have a second owner verify it. Until then, sandbox integration remains UNBOUND.

Automation extension, narrowed to the pivot

The Aug 10–28 automation plan is retained as a maintenance layer for these eight contracts. It no longer sends interns toward the retired claim backlog.

Week 9 · Expectations first

Each lane writes “healthy,” metric, exact gate, consumer, and failure status before product fixes. The machine-readable registry points to candidate-specific artifacts.

Week 10 · Propose only

Each lane wraps its gate with a kill-flag-aware maintainer. It can detect drift and prepare a proposal, but it cannot merge, deploy, mutate production, change settings, or message externally.

Week 11 · Bounded umbrella

Join registry read, scoped dispatch, evidence aggregation, and a local digest. External delivery remains an owner-only step.

Proof

Run for at least 48 hours, inject a synthetic regression, prove detection and proposal generation, rerun the gate, and record a canonical status without auto-merging.
