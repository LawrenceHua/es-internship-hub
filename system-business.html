Source trail

Where to inspect.

Business evidence lives across plans, product behavior and delivery receipts. Code is one input, never the whole answer.

  • docs/MLOPS_RUNBOOK.md and .github/workflows/Operational guidance and workflow definitions in the private default branch. A document or workflow file does not prove execution.
  • backend/server/routes/pricing.pyPricing behavior in source. It does not prove willingness to pay or an active commercial offer.
  • Read the one-founder build guideThe reusable pattern: direct agents, verify evidence and keep consequential actions human-owned.
  • Inspect the program ledgerA public record of work and the evidence attached to each claim.
  • Follow a change from sentence to receiptThe existing teaching route through a bounded brief, agent lane, review and release desk.

Automation and tests

Automate coordination, not accountability.

  • Agents can inventory evidence, propose priorities and prepare bounded briefs.The founder supplies the conscious rationale when a change alters product ground truth.
  • Delivery gates test whether work matches its contract.Tests, CI, previews and receipts answer different questions and must not be collapsed into one green status.
  • Business results need denominators and provider evidence.A plan, page, feature flag or release candidate does not establish demand, usage, revenue or impact.

Critical boundary

Agents recommend. The founder owns consequence.

Autonomy is a designed delegation system. It works when inputs, permissions, outputs and stopping conditions are explicit and when evidence can overturn the plan.

Never delegateThe rationale for a material product change, spending authority, public representation, user consent or an irreversible release decision.
Delegate wellResearch, implementation, tests, comparisons, monitoring and draft recommendations with exact evidence contracts.
